Research line 01

Hydrogen Systems

Design, siting and integration of green hydrogen production and its derivatives, from single plants to industrial parks.

Green Hydrogen Design & Capacity Expansion

Process design and capacity-expansion planning for green hydrogen that integrates economic and operational autonomy objectives. Industrial & Engineering Chemistry Research (2024).

  • Published ↗
  • Process design
  • Capacity planning

GIS-AHP Land Suitability for Hydrogen in Chile

A spatial suitability framework for green hydrogen and its derivatives that combines GIS with the Analytic Hierarchy Process and tests sensitivity to export-certification rules. International Journal of Hydrogen Energy.

  • Published
  • GIS
  • Multi-criteria decision analysis
  • Hydrogen siting

H₂–Methanol–Ammonia Industrial Park

Multi-agent design of an industrial park that balances economics with each participant's operational autonomy, and selects the hydrogen carrier simultaneously.

  • In preparation
  • Multi-objective optimization

Research line 02

Energy Governance

Public policy and decision frameworks for deploying hydrogen at national scale, developed with Chile's Ministry of Energy.

Green Hydrogen Action Plan 2023–2030

Chile's Green Hydrogen Action Plan (Plan de Acción de Hidrógeno Verde 2023–2030), the Ministry of Energy's roadmap for developing a national green hydrogen industry.

  • Energy policy
  • National strategy

Hydrogen Strategies for Chile

Evaluation and investment framework, investment guidelines and an inter-agency decision-making framework for nationwide hydrogen deployment, developed at the University of Chile Energy Center for the Energy Ministry.

  • Investment frameworks
  • Public instruments

Green Hydrogen: Impacts and Responsibilities in Land Use

A mathematical model that ranks land suitability for hydrogen production across Chile, together with technical, environmental and socioeconomic criteria to assess the impacts of hydrogen projects.

  • Land use
  • Impact assessment

Research line 03

Multi-Agent Systems Optimization

Game theory and reinforcement learning for production networks where several decision-makers share infrastructure. Core of my Ph.D. thesis.

Distributed RL for Decentralized Process Networks

Reinforcement learning agents that each design their own part of a chemical production network, combined with game-theoretical models of how those agents interact. Presented at CAPE Forum 2026.

  • In preparation
  • Multi-agent RL
  • Game theory
  • Decentralized design

CO₂-to-SAF: Game-Theoretic Techno-Economics

Strategic design of CO₂-reuse pathways for sustainable aviation fuel through gasification, reforming and Fischer–Tropsch synthesis, with several decision-makers along the value chain. Presented at ESCAPE36 (2026).

  • In preparation
  • Techno-economic analysis
  • Sustainable aviation fuel
  • Fischer–Tropsch
