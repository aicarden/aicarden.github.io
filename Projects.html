Projects

Research and industry projects spanning multi-agent process design, hydrogen systems, sustainable fuels, and operations optimization.

Research Projects

Distributed RL for Decentralized Process Networks

Reinforcement learning agents that each design their own part of a chemical production network, combined with game-theoretical models of how those agents interact. Core of my Ph.D. thesis; presented at CAPE Forum 2026.

  • Multi-agent RL
  • Game theory
  • Decentralized design

CO₂-to-SAF: Game-Theoretic Techno-Economics

Strategic design of CO₂-reuse pathways for sustainable aviation fuel through gasification, reforming and Fischer–Tropsch synthesis. Presented at ESCAPE36 (2026).

  • TEA
  • Optimization
  • Sustainable aviation fuel

H₂–Methanol–Ammonia Industrial Park

Multi-agent design of an industrial park that balances economics with each participant's operational autonomy, and selects the hydrogen carrier simultaneously.

  • Multi-objective optimization
  • Hydrogen carriers

Green Hydrogen Design & Capacity Expansion

Process design and capacity-expansion planning for green hydrogen that integrates economic and operational autonomy objectives. Published in I&EC Research (2024).

  • Process design
  • Capacity planning
  • Published ↗

GIS-AHP Land Suitability for Hydrogen in Chile

A spatial suitability framework for green hydrogen and its derivatives that combines GIS with the Analytic Hierarchy Process and tests sensitivity to export-certification rules. Published in the International Journal of Hydrogen Energy.

  • GIS
  • Multi-criteria decision analysis
  • Hydrogen siting

Hydrogen Strategies for Chile

Evaluation and investment framework, investment guidelines and an inter-agency decision-making framework for nationwide hydrogen deployment, developed for Chile's Energy Ministry.

  • Energy policy
  • Investment frameworks

Industry & Consulting Projects

Predictive CaO Feed Control

A predictive model for calcium oxide feed in a pulp mill's lime kiln and caustification area, built from discrete upstream measurements of concentration, temperature and flow. Celulosa Arauco, 2022.

  • Predictive modelling
  • Process control

Batch Scheduling Software

Weekly production-scheduling optimization that reduces storage use while meeting demand. Vegusta, 2020–2021.

  • Scheduling
  • Optimization

Refrigeration Control Optimization

  • Control
  • Energy efficiency

Hospital Patient Scheduling

  • Operations research
  • Healthcare
