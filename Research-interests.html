PhD Research

Carnegie Mellon University, Department of Chemical Engineering · Advisor: Prof. Ana I. Torres

Chemical production networks are rarely owned by one decision-maker. A hydrogen producer, a methanol plant and a fuel refinery may share land, utilities and pipelines, yet each one has its own economics and its own goals. My doctoral research develops game-theoretical and reinforcement learning frameworks to design these multi-agent process systems, that account for the interests of every participant.

Research Lines

01

Multi-Agent Chemical Process Design with Game Theory and Reinforcement Learning

The core of my thesis. I formulate process design as a problem with several interacting agents and combine game-theoretical models with reinforcement learning, including distributed RL for the decentralized design of chemical production networks.

  • Game theory
  • Distributed RL
  • Decentralized design
  • Speaker, CAPE Forum 2026
02

Strategic Design of CO₂-Reuse Pathways for Sustainable Aviation Fuel

A game-theoretic techno-economic analysis of producing sustainable aviation fuel from CO₂. The framework covers syngas production through gasification and reforming, followed by Fischer–Tropsch synthesis, with several decision-makers along the value chain. Joint work with Víctor Soria-Alcaide.

  • Techno-economic analysis
  • Fischer–Tropsch
  • CO₂ utilization
  • Speaker, ESCAPE36
03

Multi-Agent H₂–MeOH–NH₃ Industrial Parks

Design of industrial parks where hydrogen, methanol and ammonia producers coexist. The model optimizes economics and each participant's autonomy together, and selects the hydrogen carrier at the same time as the design.

  • Multi-objective optimization
  • Hydrogen carriers
  • Industrial parks
04

Foundations: Green Hydrogen Design and Capacity Expansion

Earlier work, published in Industrial & Engineering Chemistry Research, on green hydrogen process design and capacity expansion that integrates economic and operational autonomy objectives. It is the starting point for the multi-agent view I develop in my Ph.D.

  • Process design
  • Capacity expansion
  • Green hydrogen

Selected coursework at CMU: Advanced Process Systems Engineering (Prof. Ignacio Grossmann), Large-Scale Mathematical Methods (Prof. Lorenz T. Biegler), and Advanced Topics in Machine Learning and Game Theory (Prof. Fei Fang).
