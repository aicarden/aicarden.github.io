About Me

Bienvenid@s!

I studied Chemical Engineering at the University of Chile (FCFM), where I finished my Bachelor's, Professional Degree and M.Sc. During my years at FCFM I specialized in the engineering fundamentals of process systems design and optimization, participating in industrial internships and projects of direct engineering relevance and application to industry.

I am now a Ph.D. student in Chemical Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Prof. Ana I. Torres. I develop game-theoretical and reinforcement learning frameworks for systems engineering problems, with a special focus on cases where several companies or plants share infrastructure and each one pursues its own goals. The main applications of my work are in renewable energy, green hydrogen and its derivatives such as methanol, ammonia and synthetic hydrocarbons produced from CO₂.

Before my Ph.D., I worked across public policy, heavy industry, university teaching and consulting. With this expertise, I strive to develop solutions that not only depend on data or theory, but are founded on a strong engineering background.
